## 🎯 THE PROBLEM

### Current State: "Point-in-Time" Audits
Most enterprise audits (SOC2, HIPAA, PCI-DSS, ISO 27001) are **"Point-in-Time"** assessments:
- ✅ System verified as compliant **on audit day**
- ❌ **10 minutes later**: Configuration change breaks compliance
- ❌ **Nobody knows** until next audit cycle (6-12 months)
- ❌ Companies spend **2-4 weeks** preparing for each audit
- ❌ **Reactive** approach: Fix issues after they're found

### Real-World Example
**Scenario**: S3 bucket accidentally made public after audit
- **Traditional Approach**: Discovered 6 months later during next audit
- **Cost**: Potential data breach, regulatory fines, reputation damage
- **AuditAura Approach**: Alert within seconds, fixed within minutes

### Market Pain Points
1. **Audit Prep Panic**: Teams scramble for weeks before audits
2. **Compliance Drift**: Systems fall out of compliance between audits
3. **Manual Evidence Collection**: Hours spent gathering proof
4. **No Visibility**: Can't answer "Are we compliant right now?"
5. **Reactive Fixes**: Problems discovered too late
